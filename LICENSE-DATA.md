under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Licence (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

You may share and adapt the data for any purpose, provided you give appropriate credit by citing
this archive (see README.md), link to the licence, and indicate if changes were made.

Third-party data keep their own terms: IBTrACS (NOAA NCEI) in `data/ibtracs/`; Landsat (USGS),
from which `data/shoreline/` is derived; GADM boundaries and Bangladesh Meteorological Department
records, which are not redistributed here.
