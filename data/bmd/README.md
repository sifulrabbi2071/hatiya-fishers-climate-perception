# Meteorological data (not included)

Monthly highest maximum temperature, lowest minimum temperature and total rainfall for the
Hatiya station were obtained on request from the Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD),
Climate Division, Agargaon, Dhaka. BMD data are not redistributed here.

To reproduce Section 2.5.1, request the station records from BMD and place the three CSV
exports in this folder with these names (the format is the standard BMD Climate Division export):

- `Monthly_Highest_Maximum_Temperature_6_.csv` (2000–2025)
- `Monthly_Lowest_Minimum_Temperature_5_.csv` (2000–2025)
- `Monthly_Total_Rainfall_11_.csv` (1999–2025)

The resulting trend statistics are reported in the supplementary material of the manuscript (Table S1).
